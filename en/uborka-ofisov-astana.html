Office cleaning in Astana

Choose maintenance care or a more thorough deep clean for an office. The format depends on the floor area, the property's condition and the spaces that need attention.

Tasks to discuss

  • ✓Accessible surfaces, floors, skirting boards, doors and handles.
  • ✓Kitchen areas: cabinet exteriors and the agreed list of tasks.
  • ✓Washrooms, sanitary fittings, mirrors and accessible surfaces.
  • ✓Separately, windows, fabric chairs, armchairs and other upholstered furniture.

Cleaning arrangements and office access

Tell us whether you need a single visit or regular maintenance, and share your preferred time. Frequency, availability and access must be agreed beforehand. Identify restricted rooms or surfaces and explain access rules for workstations. Discuss preparation of personal belongings, documents and equipment before cleaning starts.

What affects the price

An initial assessment needs the floor area, photos, condition and a proposed task list. Maintenance and deep cleaning have different scopes. Windows, appliance interiors and upholstery cleaning are not automatically included. The final price and scope are agreed before work; the online estimate remains a guide.

Prices are estimates. We confirm the work scope and final price before cleaning begins.
