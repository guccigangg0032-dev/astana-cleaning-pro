What's included in your cleaning

A clear distinction between standard work and paid extras. We confirm the scope and safe cleaning methods before your appointment.

Deep cleaning

Included in the service

Full checklist for accessible surfaces suitable for cleaning. Cupboards need to be emptied in advance or assistance arranged.

  • ✓Dry and damp cleaning of all accessible surfaces
  • ✓Inside cupboards and shelves if emptied first
  • ✓Chandeliers and light fixtures where safely accessible
  • ✓Walls and isolated marks where suitable for cleaning
  • ✓Ceilings where materials and conditions permit safe cleaning
  • ✓Balcony: floors, sills and reachable surfaces (excluding windows)
  • ✓Kitchen cupboards outside and inside if emptied
  • ✓External accessible surfaces of electric and gas hobs
  • ✓Bathroom fixtures, mirrors and reachable surfaces
  • ✓Floors, skirting boards, doors, handles and windowsills

Maintenance (wet) cleaning

Included in the service

Routine cleaning without intensive removal of heavy or long-standing dirt. Ideal for ongoing upkeep.

  • ✓Damp-wiping accessible surfaces
  • ✓Mopping floors
  • ✓Vacuuming floor coverings
  • ✓Wiping skirting boards
  • ✓Doors, frames and door handles
  • ✓Dry wiping external socket and switch covers, without moisture
  • ✓Bathrooms: bath, toilet and washing machine exterior
  • ✓Mirrors
  • ✓Inside surface of the front door
  • ✓Kitchen cupboard exteriors and backsplash
  • ✓Balcony: floor and windowsill
  • ✓Radiator exteriors
  • ✓Windowsills

Priced separately

  • ✓Window cleaning — from 3,000 ₸
  • ✓Inside oven and microwave — appliance cleaning from 3,000 ₸ per service; final quote on request
  • ✓Chair upholstery cleaning — quoted by quantity and fabric
  • ✓Curtain washing and ironing — on request; removal and rehanging arranged separately

Ceilings, walls, light fixtures, hard-to-reach areas and delicate finishes are cleaned only after assessing the surface and safe access. Wet cleaning cannot be promised for every material. Curtains are washed or ironed only when fabric and care labels allow. Take-down and rehanging are arranged separately.
