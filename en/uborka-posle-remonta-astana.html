Post-renovation cleaning in Astana

Renovation leaves construction dust and traces of finishing work. This service has a separately agreed scope based on the materials, the property's condition and what can be safely cleaned.

What to share for an assessment

  • ✓The total floor area and the rooms where renovation has finished.
  • ✓Photos of dust and renovation residue on floors, doors, sills and other surfaces.
  • ✓Details of finishes and recently applied materials, where known.
  • ✓Whether furniture or leftover materials are present, and any access restrictions.

Agreeing the scope

The focus is removing construction dust and accessible renovation residue from suitable surfaces. Difficult marks and sensitive finishes need assessment before a method is chosen. Request window washing separately: the number of windows and access affect the estimate. Discuss remaining bulky waste or building materials in advance; their removal is not automatically included.

Estimate and site readiness

Select post-renovation cleaning and the floor area on the prices page for an initial guide. It is not a fixed offer for every condition. The final work, extras and price are agreed before cleaning starts. Let us know if renovation is continuing, as this may affect preparation and scheduling.

Prices are estimates. We confirm the work scope and final price before cleaning begins.

Service questions

Can every renovation mark be removed?+

That depends on the material and the residue. Complete removal of every mark cannot be promised without assessing the surface.

Does the service include window washing?+

Window washing is a separate service. Share the number of windows, glazing type and access conditions when enquiring.

Can cleaning start immediately after renovation?+

Tell us which works are finished and which materials were used. Finish readiness, access and the date must be agreed; we do not assign one drying time to all materials.
