Services

Apartment cleaning in Astana

Choose a cleaning format to match your apartment and the task. Maintenance cleaning helps keep everyday order, deep cleaning covers more thorough work, and a recently renovated apartment needs a separately agreed scope.

Choose the right cleaning format

  • ✓Maintenance cleaning: wiping accessible surfaces, cleaning floors, kitchen exteriors and the bathroom without intensive removal of difficult deposits.
  • ✓Deep cleaning: thorough care for accessible surfaces that can be safely cleaned, including cabinet interiors when emptied.
  • ✓Post-renovation cleaning: construction dust and renovation residue, subject to assessment of materials and the apartment's condition.

Information for your estimate

Share the floor area, district in Astana, cleaning format and main concerns. Photos of the kitchen, bathroom and difficult areas help clarify the work. If only part of the apartment needs cleaning, list those rooms. The final price and scope are agreed before work starts.

Extras and preparation

Window washing, upholstery cleaning, cleaning inside ovens and microwaves, and curtain washing or ironing are agreed separately. Empty cabinets before interior cleaning or discuss assistance. Walls, ceilings and light fittings are assessed for material compatibility and safe access.

Prices are estimates. We confirm the work scope and final price before cleaning begins.
