Deep cleaning in Astana

Deep cleaning suits a property that needs more thorough care than regular maintenance. Cleaning methods are selected for the material, condition and safe accessibility of each surface.

What the service covers

  • ✓Dry and damp cleaning of accessible surfaces, floors, skirting boards, doors, handles and window sills.
  • ✓Kitchen cabinet exteriors and emptied interiors; accessible outer surfaces of electric or gas cookers.
  • ✓Sanitary fittings, mirrors and accessible bathroom surfaces.
  • ✓The insides of cabinets and shelves after belongings have been removed.
  • ✓Balcony floors, sills and accessible surfaces; window washing is separate.

Delicate surfaces and safe access

Walls, ceilings and light fittings are cleaned only when the finish permits it and access is safe. Water and cleaning products are not suitable for every finish. Share photos of delicate materials and difficult marks before agreeing the scope.

Separately agreed work

Window washing, upholstery cleaning, oven and microwave interiors, and curtain washing or ironing are not automatically included. Curtain care depends on the fabric and care label; taking curtains down and rehanging them is agreed separately. Service questions

Should I empty cabinets?+

Cabinets and shelves must be emptied for interior cleaning. Discuss any help you need before the visit.

Can all walls and ceilings be washed?+

That depends on the finish and access. We do not promise wet cleaning of every material without assessment.

How does deep cleaning differ from maintenance cleaning?+

Maintenance cleaning keeps everyday cleanliness without intensive removal of difficult deposits. Deep cleaning has a more thorough scope, including agreed interiors of emptied cabinets.
