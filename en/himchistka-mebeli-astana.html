Upholstery cleaning in Astana

Furniture treatment is selected after assessing the upholstery and soiling. Material, dimensions, condition and previous care matter for sofas, armchairs, mattresses and fabric-covered chairs.

Furniture to include in your enquiry

  • ✓Sofas: number of seats, straight or corner layout, dimensions and upholstery type.
  • ✓Armchairs: size, material and affected areas.
  • ✓Mattresses: dimensions, surface condition and the nature of the soiling.
  • ✓Fabric chairs: quantity and the covering on seats and backs.

Request a preliminary assessment

Send an overall photo and close-ups of stains. Share the care label if available and tell us about previous cleaning attempts. This helps assess suitability and limitations. The final price depends on the item, size, fabric and soiling, with conditions agreed before treatment.

Realistic expectations

An old stain, colour change or damaged material is not the same as removable dirt. Complete removal of stains or odours is not guaranteed in advance. Delicate or unidentified fabrics require assessment. Upholstery cleaning is not automatically included in apartment cleaning, so list each item when enquiring.

Prices are estimates. We confirm the work scope and final price before cleaning begins.

Service questions

Can I get a price by simply saying 'sofa'?+

A useful estimate needs the size, number of seats, layout, fabric and photos of the condition. The prices page offers starting guides; the final amount follows assessment.

Will every stain and odour disappear?+

We do not promise that in advance. Results depend on the material, the type and age of the soiling, previous treatment and upholstery condition.

When can I use the furniture again?+

Aftercare and any use restrictions depend on the material and selected method. One drying time is not promised for every item.
