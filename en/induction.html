SURFACE TREATMENT ASSESSMENT

Induction cooktop surfaces

We accept enquiries for glass-ceramic hob care and possible cosmetic treatment, but we are still seeking specialist partners. Availability and suitable methods require individual assessment.

Important before enquiring

Manufacturers warn that genuine scratches on glass-ceramic surfaces often cannot be polished away, while abrasive treatment may damage glass or control markings. We cannot promise scratch removal, a like-new finish or service availability before assessing the appliance. Cracked surfaces must not be treated.
