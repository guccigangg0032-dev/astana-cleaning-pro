Window cleaning in Astana

Window washing can be arranged separately from cleaning a property. The estimate needs the quantity, dimensions, glazing type and information about safe access to the surfaces.

Information for an assessment

  • ✓An overall photo of the windows, balcony glazing or large glazed panels.
  • ✓The number and approximate size of sections, including fixed and opening parts.
  • ✓Which sides need cleaning and whether they can be reached from inside.
  • ✓Any renovation residue, stickers, old deposits or sensitive finishes.

Safe access and difficult glazing

Exterior cleaning, work at height and hard-to-reach areas need individual assessment. We cannot confirm cleaning of every exterior surface in advance. Send photos of the construction and describe access so the available scope can be agreed. Frame and glass materials also affect the method.

Windows are separately priced

Including balcony floors and accessible surfaces in a cleaning order does not automatically include the glazing. Starting guidance is on the prices page; the final amount depends on quantity, size, soiling and access. Add windows to your estimate or describe a separate task through WhatsApp.

Prices are estimates. We confirm the work scope and final price before cleaning begins.

Service questions

Is window washing included in deep cleaning?+

No. It is separately priced. Tell us which windows you need, their quantity and access conditions.

Can you clean exterior windows on any floor?+

Exterior and high-level work must be assessed for safe access. Such work is not confirmed without that assessment.

How are large glazed areas assessed?+

Send photos, dimensions, the number of sections and details of which parts open. Unusual or difficult-to-reach glazing is assessed individually.
